◆ apply_gravity()

void robcraft::engine::world::apply_gravity ( double & y,
double & vy,
double rest_y,
double gravity,
double dt )

Integrates one fixed-timestep tick of vertical motion under gravity.

Parameters
yIn/out vertical world position (meters).
vyIn/out vertical velocity in m/s (positive = up).
rest_yGround height the entity rests on (its resting Y).
gravityAcceleration magnitude in m/s^2 (must be >= 0).
dtFixed timestep in seconds.

If the entity is on the ground (y <= rest_y) with no upward velocity it is pinned to rest_y with vy = 0. Otherwise gravity accelerates it downward and a ground crossing on this tick clamps it to rest_y with vy = 0. Deterministic: depends only on its arguments.
